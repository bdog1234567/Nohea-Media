I'm Braden Di Mauro, a VFX artist and AI filmmaker. By day I'm a Creative Director at Meta. Nohea Media is where I put the work that's mine: spec campaigns, VFX shots and generative stills.

My rule is simple. If a real photographer couldn't have shot it, I pull it back. Rain, fog, wet streets, one hard light, a contained fire: all the drama, none of the tells.

I came up in compositing and color, so the AI sits inside a real pipeline. Roto, tracking and comps that used to take days now take hours, and the time goes back into the frame.
